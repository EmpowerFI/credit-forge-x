# Demo script: one economic loop

This script follows one story end to end: a sponsor's evidence, credit intelligence, a qualified opportunity, capital, repayment and outcome, then back to the sponsor. It runs on the hackathon environment at `www.empowerfi.io/app`. The login page enters each demo account with one click — nobody types a password on camera.

The bar at the top of the app numbers the three stories: 1 Impact Intelligence, 2 Credit & Capital Engine, 3 Investor Console. Operations sit beside them. On the right, **View platform as** switches between the five views (sponsor, investor or impact fund, operator, community, entrepreneur): each opens a page with that view's value and tools. A demo account switches to the right demo persona in one click, so a judge never has to sign out.

> Prototype of a future regulated P2P productive-credit architecture. Hackathon investments, returns, FX and Pix settlement are simulated; blockchain transactions use test assets on Devnet.

**Optional, after step 7 (30 s):** the **Settlement route** card, on the engine, when the global pool wins: the two ways her dollars become reais, side by side, each leading with what reaches her Pix. "Global capital won, so the dollars have to become reais. One conversion at the payout, or reais bought at allocation and paid out one to one. The card shows what each leaves in her account and what the vault must release to deliver her whole principal — and **Why this route?** shows the comparison line by line." The three seeded cases are in the demo investor's portfolio: direct winning, the stablecoin winning because the rate it locked beats today's, and the stablecoin unavailable when a loan settled.

**Optional, after step 11 (30 s):** in **Written in reais, funded in dollars**, **Open the proof** → the **Settlement route** proof, with its routing block: the route, when it was quoted, the net it was quoted at, the provider's rate card, the reason and the comparator's version; then **Verify on Solana**. "What Solana keeps is the route and a hash of the whole decision — both quotes, their costs and the reasons. Never a provider, an amount or a rate. And no stablecoin transaction was made, so none is shown: this proves a decision was taken, not that a token moved."

**Optional, after step 7 (60 s) — the capital network, and the second question:** in the left rail of the engine, **Capital Network**. Six routes from five invented providers: a regional credit product, a microcredit operator, a productive exchange network that is never described as a loan, the two P2P pools, and an international impact fund. Each card shows the policy it is offered under — ticket, states, purposes, term, reported history, papers — and every route belonging to someone else says *Partner approval required*. "The pool engine asks one question: which of our two pools can fund this. The network asks a wider one — what else exists, and under what terms. It recommends; it never approves."

Then the run the seed leaves for the camera: the largest request no pool could fund at all. The seed prints its reference at the end of a run, as *left unrouted for the live run*. **Select it → Run the capital engine.** R$ 6,700 asked, R$ 4,000 that local routes reach, and an **external capital gap** of R$ 2,700. Under the plan, **Capital from outside Brazil**: the gap, her cost as the pool engine prices it, her cost once a rail has actually quoted the conversion, the instalment that gap would add against what is left of her month, and the questions it had to pass to get there. "Two answers from one run. The first is what the network can do. The second is asked only of what local capital could not reach, and it is a different question — not whether she is good for it, but whether money that crosses a border reaches her without costing her more than she can carry. A gap that never existed is not asked at all."

Then the second act. **What the network refused** names the impact fund: *papers missing*. Under *What she has on file*, tick **CNPJ or MEI registration** and **Three months of bank statements**, and run it again → **decision 2**, and the fund takes the R$ 2,700. "Nothing about her changed. An operator stated two papers she has, and a route that was shut opened. Both answers are recorded, so a recommendation can be read against what the engine was told — and running it twice on the same facts records nothing and says so."

**Optional, after step 7 (60 s):** **Operating economics** on the engine page. "Can productive credit become cheaper to operate without becoming weaker credit? We don't claim the answer. We measure it: cost to serve beside eligibility discipline, time to decision beside affordability checks, follow-up, and portfolio quality. The rates are pilot assumptions and the data is simulated."

Then **Cost to mobilize capital**, on the same page: what crossed the border, what moving it cost, and the rate per R$ 100 mobilised — set beside the cost to serve per R$ 100 lent, and never added to it. "Here is what it costs to bring that money in, next to what it costs us to serve the loan. Two costs, two denominators, and a hedge that belongs to neither — a required return on currency risk is not a fee paid to a rail. The database is built so the two cannot merge: their rates live in different tables, and no cost event can carry a mobilisation stage." The **eligible external capital gap** on the same panel is what the second question has said yes to and nobody has funded yet; **global funding coverage** is the share of everything lent here that was raised abroad.

**Optional, after step 9 (40 s) — the Zcash track:** the same investment, paid privately, and then handed to the auditor to check without us.

*The payment.* In the invest panel choose **ZEC blindado** instead of **USDC da Devnet**. No wallet is connected and none is needed. The panel answers with EmpowerFI's shielded address, the amount in ZEC at the quote it just locked, a memo carrying a random reference, and a QR. Pay it from any Zcash wallet. The four steps then move on their own: *paid* → *seen in a Zcash block* → *converted to USDC and credited to the vault* → *allocated and proven on Solana*. "She has no Solana wallet, and she does not need one. She pays a shielded address on Zcash. The amount, the memo, the sender and the recipient are all encrypted — and what reaches Solana is the operator crediting the vault, so no investor wallet is ever linked to that inflow."

*The check.* **Operations → Audit → Zcash**. The treasury's **viewing key**, with **Reveal** and **Copy**, and under it the three commands that import it into `zcash-devtool` and list the same payments. "We can read our own treasury because we hold its viewing key. So can an auditor — here it is, and here is how to list these same notes without trusting anything on this screen. It reads everything and can spend nothing. That is selective disclosure, not 'trust our dashboard'."

*If a judge clicks the explorer link and lands on "Transaction Not Found",* that is the demonstration, not a fault, and the screen says so beside the link: a shielded transaction shows no amount, no memo and no addresses, an explorer can only say one exists, and testnet explorers often lag or skip the newest shielded pool. The block height beside it came from our own watcher, which reads blocks and never the mempool — a note it recorded is a note that was in a block.

*Timing.* Testnet block intervals are irregular: a payment has taken 3 minutes and it can take longer. Pay ten minutes before recording and show the finished state, or pay live and cut to the audit console while it confirms. Do not stand in front of the four steps waiting.

**Optional prelude:** `maria@demo.empowerfi.io` submits her September check-in and asks for capital, and it becomes a qualified opportunity (Operations → My business).

## Before recording

- **Choose the language.** From the English site (or with the EN | PT switch) the app reads in English; from `empowerfi.io/pt`, in Portuguese. The demo data is the same in both.
- **Reseed the hackathon environment:** run `seed-demo-accounts.mts`, then `seed-demo.mts --yes`, then `seed-positions.mts --investor-wallet <the wallet you will connect in step 9>`. Together they use about 1.2 devnet SOL.
  - The seed prints the capital figures, the program, the loan it leaves unmeasured for step 15, and the code of the opportunity it leaves **USDC 5 short** for step 9.
  - It also prints the capital network's run: how many plans it recorded, how much gap the second question found eligible, and the request it left **unrouted for the live run**. One request is left without the business papers on file, so its plan shows a local route that a paper would reopen.
  - **Two references name one request.** The operator's picker shows a partner the *borrower's* reference (P-…); the investor console shows the *opportunity's* code (Q-…). The seed prints capital-network cases as P-… and investment cases as Q-…, which is what each screen searches by.
  - `seed-positions.mts` admits one wallet per investor and hands each its assets; the mint cron then creates them on devnet, five a minute, so 36 take about eight minutes. Without `--investor-wallet` the wallet you connect in step 9 is not admitted, and the asset in step 12 never appears.
  - Proofs take about half an hour to confirm on devnet after a reseed.
- **Settlement cases** are seeded on global loans and appear in the demo investor's portfolio: two of the direct route, one of the stablecoin winning, one of the stablecoin unavailable. The seed prints them at the end of a run.
- **Case codes** (Q-…) change with every reseed. The engine's picker searches by code, purpose and amount.
- **For the Zcash optional:** a Zcash **testnet** wallet with a little TAZ (the faucet gives 0.1 TAZ a day), and the treasury's viewing key already set on the project. Check `Audit → Zcash` reads *up to* a block within a few of the chain tip before you record — if the watcher is behind, payments will not appear.
- **Wallet:** have a devnet wallet with test SOL and at least USDC 5 ready for step 9, and pass its address to `seed-positions.mts`. The invest panel links to the faucets.
- **Check the assets before recording:** open *Positions* as the demo investor. Every asset should show a mint address; if one says "ainda não criado", the mint cron has not reached it yet or the operator wallet is dry — the same wallet the anchor queue uses.
